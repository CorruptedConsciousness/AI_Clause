# AI Assistance & Development Disclosure

## Purpose

This document establishes a transparent disclosure policy for software projects developed and maintained by this repository's owner.

Artificial intelligence is used as a development, educational, and problem-solving tool. Its involvement is acknowledged openly rather than concealed.

## How AI Is Used

Depending on the project, AI assistance may include:

- Generating initial code, functions, or complete components.
- Debugging, troubleshooting, and suggesting improvements.
- Explaining programming concepts and development practices.
- Assisting with software architecture and project planning.
- Producing or refining technical documentation.
- Reviewing existing code for errors and potential improvements.

The extent of AI involvement varies between projects.

## Human Involvement and Responsibility

The repository owner directs project goals, determines requirements, integrates components, conducts testing, and makes development decisions.

AI-generated code is not automatically considered correct, secure, or production-ready.

Projects may contain code written independently, code written with AI guidance, and code substantially generated by AI.

## Transparency

The use of AI is not presented as independent human authorship.

AI assistance does not guarantee software quality, correctness, security, or fitness for a particular purpose.

## Continuing Development

As programming knowledge and experience increase, AI-assisted components may be reviewed, refactored, rewritten, or replaced.

The objective is to develop reliable software while continually improving independent programming ability.
