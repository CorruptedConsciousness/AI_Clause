# AI Clause

### AI-Assisted Software Development, Transparency & Accountability

This repository establishes a centralized disclosure policy and development standards for my software projects.

I am a programming student and independent developer who uses artificial intelligence as part of my development workflow.

My projects range from independently written educational exercises to experimental software systems developed with substantial AI assistance.

I believe AI-assisted development is a legitimate approach to building software, provided its use is acknowledged transparently and the resulting work is represented accurately.

## Development Philosophy

My approach emphasizes:

- **Transparency:** Acknowledging AI-generated and AI-assisted contributions.
- **Education:** Using AI to support learning while developing independent programming skills.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Revisiting and improving projects as my technical abilities develop.

AI is a tool within my development process, not a substitute for understanding, testing, or responsible decision-making.

## Scope

These policies apply to all software repositories.

Individual repositories may include additional disclosures describing their particular use of AI.

These documents describe my development practices and goals. They do not constitute a certification of software quality or security.

## A Note on Authorship

I do not claim to have independently written code that was generated by artificial intelligence.

My objective is to build useful software, improve my technical competence, and maintain an honest record of my development.
